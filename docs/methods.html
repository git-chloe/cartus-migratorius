Research notes / Quality assurance

Method before myth.

I kept the peculiar field-journal narrative. The calculations, data provenance, assumptions and limitations remain open for inspection.

01 / The research population

What was counted?

The notebook uses a privately preserved 2025 extract of the City of Ottawa's public 311 service-request records. The original CSV produced 372,069 parseable service requests under the recorded import settings; one malformed CSV line was skipped with a parser warning. English descriptions were extracted from bilingual fields and searched for “Shopping Cart,” resulting in 411 related service requests.

411original shopping-cart service requests
247reports with both latitude and longitude
214distinct addresses among the geocoded reports
164unmappable reports, concentrated by service family

For frequency analysis, I counted individual reports. For neighbourhood proximity, I used distinct address locations. For repeat-report sensitivity tests, I applied a provisional episode heuristic. These denominators are not interchangeable.

02 / The analysis trail

Decisions, not just destinations.

DATA INTEGRITY

Source fingerprint

The saved 2025 CSV has SHA-256 e85a4dce…5ec6c48f1a. Its full value appears in the notebook and source notes. The rolling “last year” download URL must not be treated as a permanent 2025 archive.

MISSING DATA

The Passport Office

All 104 Property Standards reports lacked public coordinates. Park Maintenance and City Building Maintenance had full coordinate coverage; Road Maintenance was partially mapped and varied by season.

TIME

The Time Machine

Unusual 2026 closure-date concentrations affected records opened throughout 2025. I did not interpret administrative closure dates as verified cart-removal dates.

REPEATED REPORTING

A 21-day rule

A provisional episode groups same-address reports within 21 days of the episode's first report, not the preceding report. The heuristic was tested for sensitivity and does not prove cart identity.

SPATIAL PROXIMITY

Haversine distance

Geographic comparisons use straight-line distances on the Earth’s surface. They are not walking/driving distances, cart routes or cart provenance evidence.

LATER RETAIL DATA

Candidate hearings

Exploratory candidate retailers were screened from a later 2026 Overture places extract. Potential duplicates, non-store facilities and uncertain candidates were examined with scenario analysis.

SEASONALITY

Two different stories

Summer had the most original reports (134); spring narrowly led the mapped subset (85 vs. 82 summer). The latter is not a valid estimate of the complete city's spatial seasonality.

LOCAL CLUSTER

Woodroffe witnesses

Two addresses, 225.3 metres apart with episode starts two days apart, stood out among 406 exploratory pairs. Their proximity does not establish a shared incident.

SENSITIVITY

Does the finding survive?

Removing the Woodroffe pair reduced April–May’s share of Park reports from 61.1% to 50.0%. On estimated episodes it fell from 51.7% to 48.1%.

03 / Reproducibility

The code is public. The source archive isn't bundled.

The public repository includes a lightly copyedited, output-cleared edition of the final field journal with 71 Python code cells and 22 Markdown cells (execution outputs removed), plus an independent, runnable summary script for the report/season/episode core.

The complete notebook was developed in Google Colab against a separately preserved 2025 Ottawa CSV and a 2026 places GeoJSON file in private Drive. Re-running it requires those files, compatible dependencies and updating input paths; the site’s saved interactive Folium maps are exports from the finished notebook.

Important: I have not claimed a clean end-to-end rerun of the notebook against the historical source files in this public package; those source snapshots were not supplied for this publication build.
